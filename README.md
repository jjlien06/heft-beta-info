# Heft beta

Heft is an iPhone and iPad photo-library browser that helps you find large photos and videos, sort by size, review selected items, export originals to a folder, and convert RAW images to HEIC or JPEG.

Heft does not upload your photos to a developer server. Library scans use public PhotoKit APIs and do not download iCloud-only originals. Export and conversion may ask Apple Photos to download originals when you choose those actions.

- [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md)
- [Support and beta feedback](SUPPORT.md)

Exported files are verified before the app requests deletion. Review your backups before removing irreplaceable media. iOS presents the Photos deletion confirmation.
