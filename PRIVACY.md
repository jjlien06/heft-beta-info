# Heft privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026. Developer: Jeremy Lien.

## Your photo library

Heft requests access to your Photos library to display photos and videos, measure their resource sizes, filter and sort them, export chosen originals, and save converted copies. You may grant limited access or full access, and change that choice in iOS Settings. With limited access, Heft can only manage the items you select.

Photo content, filenames, dates, dimensions, location metadata and sizes are processed on your device. Heft does not operate a server for your photos, sell your data, include advertising, or include a developer analytics or tracking SDK.

## Local preferences and exports

Heft stores a local size cache, display preferences and an optional security-scoped bookmark for a folder you choose. Apple may include local app data in device backups according to your settings. Removing the app removes its local app data; it does not remove your Photos library or exported files.

Exported originals may include their existing metadata, including location. Converted copies retain their original creation date, location and favorite status. The destination you choose may be an external drive or a cloud file provider; anyone you share those files with, and the provider you select, may have access to them.

Scanning does not request iCloud downloads. Export or conversion can request an original from Apple Photos/iCloud when you explicitly choose that operation, under your Apple account's settings. Heft does not receive your Apple ID credentials.

## Deletion and TestFlight

Heft asks Photos to delete only the items you choose and confirm. Its export-and-delete flow first verifies exported resource sizes. Failed or cancelled exports keep the originals. The operating system manages Photos deletion and Recently Deleted; review your backups before deleting important media.

Apple operates TestFlight and can provide the developer with crash reports, usage information and feedback under its terms and privacy policy. Feedback or screenshots you submit may contain information you include. Avoid sharing private photos or filenames in feedback unless you intend to.

## Contact

Use [support](SUPPORT.md) for questions, bugs or privacy requests. We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.
